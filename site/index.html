Night, properly dark

After sunset the sky hands over to twinkling stars, the odd shooting star, and aurora curtains when you least expect them.

Your phone is the remote

Open /remote on the same Wi-Fi. Add tasks, snooze alarms, switch pages — the display follows live, and vice versa.

Alarms you can't ignore

Full-screen and solid, with custom ringtones. Space to dismiss, Z to snooze, auto-snooze when you walk away.

Focus, gently

Homelab-aware

With the optional host agent, Dayboard watches the machine it lives on — and reaches out to your phone when something changes.

I'm a homelabber too. I love messing around with servers, Linux and AI tools — the kind of person whose idea of a fun evening is reprovisioning a container that was working perfectly fine.

I had a spare display lying around. A perfectly good panel with nothing to do. So I did what any reasonable hobbyist does: I gave it a job. Dayboard started as a weekend experiment and slowly became the thing I actually look at every morning.

I build things with AI for fun — vibecoding, rabbit holes, half the ideas dying in a branch and the good ones escaping into production. My production is a Proxmox box humming in the corner, but still. Dayboard is one of the escapees.

If you've got a spare screen and a soft spot for calm software, this is for you. Star it, fork it, break it, send it back better.
